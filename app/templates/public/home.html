Capital Guide

Capital & position sizing guide

General principles for how much capital and risk-per-trade typically suit different risk appetites. This is educational context, not a personal recommendation — always size positions according to your own risk tolerance.

$200(20,000 cents) Conservative

Capital Preservation

Lower risk per trade, more room to absorb drawdown

  • Smaller position sizes relative to account balance
  • Prioritizes account longevity over speed of growth
  • Better suited to volatile or uncertain conditions
  • Typically paired with lower leverage usage
$500(50,000 cents) Recommended Balanced

Steady Growth

A measured middle ground for most traders

  • Moderate position sizing with defined risk limits
  • Room to withstand normal market swings
  • Consistent lot sizing rather than reactive changes
  • A reasonable starting point while you learn a system
$1000(100,000 cents) Growth-Oriented

Accelerated

Higher risk per trade, less cushion for drawdown

  • Larger position sizes relative to account balance
  • Faster potential capital growth, faster potential loss
  • Requires closer monitoring and a higher risk tolerance
  • Best suited to capital you can afford to risk fully

This guide is for general education only and is not personalized financial advice. Leveraged trading carries a high level of risk, and position sizing should reflect your own financial situation and risk tolerance — consult a licensed financial advisor if you're unsure.
