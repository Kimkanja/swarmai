Process

How it works

From account creation to a live license key in your trading terminal.

1. Create your account

Register with your email — takes under a minute.

2. Choose a product

Browse our software, review features and pricing, and choose to buy or apply through the affiliate route where available.

3. Get your license key

Once your purchase or affiliate verification is approved, a unique license key is generated and shown on your dashboard.

4. Activate in your terminal

Enter your license key into the EA. It contacts our server to validate access, then runs.

5. Manage everything from your dashboard

View license status, referral activity, and account details at any time.
